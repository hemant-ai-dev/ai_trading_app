# Climate, ESG, and environment context for traders

Angad can answer environment-related questions using this knowledge plus free news headlines. This is educational context, not a carbon-accounting product and not financial advice.

## What “environment” means here

- Climate and weather events that can move energy, agri, insurance, and infrastructure names.
- ESG and sustainability themes (renewables, EVs, pollution regulation, carbon).
- How environmental news may tilt sentiment around Nifty sectors — not a guaranteed price path.

## How Angad uses it

1. Search this knowledge base (RAG).
2. Pull free Yahoo/RSS headlines when you ask about climate, ESG, pollution, or green energy.
3. If you also name an index or stock (Nifty 50, RELIANCE, TATAMOTORS), attach the latest **daily** free-source close and the Python analysis engine.

## Limits

- No paid climate-data vendor is connected.
- Headlines can be delayed or incomplete.
- Environment headlines do not replace OHLCV. If market data is missing, say so.
- Do not invent emissions numbers, carbon prices, or company ESG scores that tools did not return.
